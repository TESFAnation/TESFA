# Product requirements

- Name and logo: uppercase **TESFA** only. No logo mark, gradients, or artificial marketing copy.
- Design: flat navy, off-white, charcoal, generous whitespace. System, dark, and light themes.
- Platform: Android first, English first.
- Navigation: Home, Explore, Create, Profile.
- Public visitors can browse campaigns. Real email/password accounts required for creating campaigns.
- Campaigns: medical, education, emergency. Individuals and registered organizations can apply.
- Evidence: poverty certificate or other appropriate documentation. In the prototype, a labeled fictional evidence choice stands in for the upload. No actual verification occurs.
- Review: simple automated completeness checks, then human reviewer approval. Only approved campaigns appear in public listings.
- Organizer updates and reviewed evidence workflow are later enhancements.
- Donation: preview only, never collect or simulate completed payments.
- Admin: a separate, gated web dashboard.
